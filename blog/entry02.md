# Entry 2: Research & Synthesize
##### 11/16/26

## Content
After choosing playing the violin as my topic in Blog 1, I began exploring how technology is used in the music field, specifically for violinists. I wanted to find out what inventions already exist and how they help musicians with practicing, learning, and performing. I started with general research about violin technology, but I soon realized that there were many different types of innovations. To make my research more manageable, I explored software, physical gadgets, and more advanced technologies.
