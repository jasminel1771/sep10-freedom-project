# Entry 2: Research & Synthesize
##### 11/17/26

## Content
When I first began researching violin technology, I wanted to understand how modern inventions have changed the way violinists learn, practice, and perform. I already knew that instruments could be connected to technology through things like electric violins and digital tuners, but I wanted to explore more specific examples. As I continued looking into the topic, I found technologies that I had not previously known about, including applications that analyze playing and physical devices that use vibration or wireless signals.
